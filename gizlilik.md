- Apple ile girişte e-postanı gizlemeyi seçebilirsin. O zaman Apple'ın yönlendirme adresini alırız.

## 3. Yalnız cihazında kalan veriler

Bu veriler cihazından çıkmaz:

- Ana ekran widget'larının gösterdiği görevler ve etkinlikler.
- Reklam sıklığı sayacı ve "seriyi koru" ile korunan günler.
- Görünüm ve dil tercihi.

## 4. Reklamlar

- İlerleme sayfasının altında tek bir küçük reklam kartı vardır. Kart "Sponsorlu" yazar.
- Seri bozulursa isteğe bağlı bir ödüllü reklam izleyebilirsin. Bunu yalnız sen başlatırsın.
- Tam ekran reklam yoktur. İlk günlerde reklam yoktur.
- Görevlerini, etkinliklerini ve e-postanı reklam için kullanmayız ve Google'a göndermeyiz.
- Reklamları Google AdMob gösterir. Google verini [kendi politikasına](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) göre işler.
- AB, İngiltere ve İsviçre'de reklamdan önce onayını sorarız. Onayını Profil → Hakkında → Reklam gizlilik seçenekleri ile değiştirebilirsin.
- iPhone'da seni uygulamalar arasında takip etmek için izin istemeyiz. Bu yüzden Apple reklam kimliğini (IDFA) almayız.
- Android'de reklam kimliğini telefonun Ayarlar → Gizlilik → Reklamlar bölümünden silebilir veya sıfırlayabilirsin.

# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Doable ("the app") is a task and calendar app. This policy gives the information that the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Art. 13) and Turkish data protection law (KVKK, Art. 10) require.

## 1. Data controller

- Controller: Muhammet Emre Kemancı ("we").
- Contact: k.emre.k25@gmail.com

## 2. Data we collect, purpose and legal basis

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email address | Sign-in and account | Contract (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) | Supabase |
| Name (from Google/Apple sign-in or Profile) | Show your name in the app | Contract | Supabase |
| User ID | Link your data and crash reports to your account | Contract | Supabase, Firebase Crashlytics |
| Tasks, subtasks, categories, calendars, events, daily logs, settings | Run the app and sync across devices | Contract | Your device and Supabase |
| IP address and sign-in logs | Protect your account and our servers | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) | Supabase, Cloudflare |
| Bot-check signals (IP, browser and device data) | Protect sign-in from automated attacks | Legitimate interest | Cloudflare Turnstile |
| Push token | Send you notifications | Contract | Supabase, Firebase Cloud Messaging |
| Crash reports (device model, OS version, error log) | Find and fix bugs | Legitimate interest | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Ad data (advertising ID, coarse location from IP, device data, ad interactions) | Show ads, prevent ad fraud, measure ads | EU/UK/Switzerland: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). Other regions: legitimate interest | Google AdMob |

**How we collect data:** We collect data automatically through the app. You enter some of it (tasks, name). Your device and the services you use give the rest (crash reports, advertising ID, Google/Apple sign-in).

- With Google sign-in, Google gives us only your email address and name.
- With Apple sign-in, you can hide your email. We then receive Apple's relay address.

## 3. Data that stays on your device

This data does not leave your device:

- Reminders and event alerts. Your device schedules them.
- Tasks and events that the home screen widgets show.
- The ad frequency counter and the days kept with "Keep your streak".
- Appearance and language preferences.

## 4. Ads

- One small ad card sits at the bottom of the Progress page. It is labeled "Sponsored".
- If your streak breaks, you can choose to watch a rewarded ad. Only you start it.
- There are no full-screen ads. There are no ads in your first days.
- We do not use your tasks, events or email for ads, and we do not send them to Google.
- Google AdMob shows the ads. Google processes your data under [its own policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
- In the EU, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before ads. Change it in Profile → About → Ad privacy options.
- On iPhone we do not ask permission to track you across apps. So we do not receive Apple's advertising ID (IDFA).
- On Android you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

## 5. What we do not do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not ask for location (GPS), contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.

## 6. Who receives your data

Your data goes only to these providers, only to run the app:

| Provider | Role | Servers |
|---|---|---|
| Supabase | Database and sign-in | Frankfurt, Germany (EU) |
| Google Firebase | Crash reports and notifications | USA and other countries |
| Google AdMob | Ads | USA and other countries |
| Cloudflare Turnstile | Bot check | Worldwide |
| Resend | Sign-in code email | USA |
| Google, Apple | Only if you sign in with them | USA and other countries |

**International transfers:** Some providers process data outside your country. We transfer data under GDPR Chapter V and KVKK Art. 9. We rely on the providers' data processing agreements and standard contractual clauses.

We share your data with public authorities only when the law requires it.

## 7. Retention

| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Account and app data | Until you delete your account |
| Push token | Until you sign out or delete your account |
| Crash reports | Up to 90 days (Firebase) |
| Sign-in and security logs | The provider's short log period |
| Ad data | Under Google's own policy |

When you delete your account, your account and cloud data are deleted permanently. The database on your device is deleted too. Details: [Account deletion](silme).

## 8. Your rights

Under GDPR (Art. 15–22) and KVKK (Art. 11) you have these rights:

- Know if we process your data and get information about it.
- Access your data and get a copy (portability).
- Correct wrong data.
- Delete your data.
- Object to processing and restrict it.
- Withdraw your consent at any time.
- Know who receives your data.
- Ask for compensation if you suffer damage.

To use your rights:

1. Delete your account in the app: Profile → Account → Delete account.
2. For other requests, email k.emre.k25@gmail.com from your sign-in address.
3. We answer within 30 days, free of charge.

You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In Türkiye this is the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu).

## 9. Security

- All connections are encrypted (HTTPS).
- Each user can read and write only their own data (row-level security).
- The sign-in screen is protected against automated attacks.

## 10. Children

The app is not for children under 13. If your country sets a higher age of digital consent, that age applies. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we find a child's data, we delete it.

## 11. Changes

If this policy changes, we show the new date on this page. We announce important changes in the app.
